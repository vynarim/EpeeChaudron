#### Overall Impression

The three targeted fixes from the last pass all held up under independent re-verification: no repeated eyebrows, no side-stripe borders, passing contrast. The design review surfaced two things the detector can't see: a genuine interaction bug (the back-to-top button collided with a card's wishlist button on mobile) and a documentation/code mismatch (a third eyebrow instance had crept into the modal template, contradicting DESIGN.md's own "2 uses" rule).
