#### Anti-Patterns Verdict

**LLM assessment**: Does not read as AI-generated overall — real event photography carries the page, the palette is genuinely sourced from the association's own banner, copy is specific and French rather than templated filler. The one place it brushes a "tell" is the 14-card animation grid (`index.html:103-234`): identical image/title/description/heart layout repeated 14 times with zero visual differentiation or grouping.

#### Overall Impression

The site's core credibility strategy — real photography, a banner-derived palette, French copy free of template filler — works and does not read as AI slop at a glance. But the deterministic scan caught exactly the kind of scaffolding reflex the skill warns about (an eyebrow kicker repeated above every section, left-border accents used as a decorative device in 2 places) sitting underneath that otherwise-authentic surface. Combined with a cognitive-load problem at the site's single highest-stakes moment — the primary persona (a skimming municipal decision-maker) hits an undifferentiated wall of 14 animation cards before ever reaching the section written specifically to reassure them — the biggest opportunity is sequencing and restraint, not a visual overhaul.

#### What's Working

1. **The module modal's accessibility engineering** (`assets/js/main.js:319-341`) — focus moves to the close button on open, Escape/arrow-keys/focus-trap are all correctly implemented, and the wishlist toast is `aria-live` and actually announces. This is disciplined engineering, not decoration.
2. **Photography carries the page** — no stock photos or illustration crutches anywhere; matches PRODUCT.md's "photos portent l'expérience" principle exactly.
3. **The mobile bottom nav is a committed decision** — it fully replaces the hamburger menu (confirmed `display:none` on `.menu-button` at 390px) rather than running both patterns side by side.

#### Priority Issues

- **[P1] Eyebrow kicker repeated above all 6 sections + the hero**: Name the problem — the exact "tiny uppercase tracked eyebrow above every section" pattern the skill calls the single most saturated AI-scaffold tell, confirmed by the detector at 7 instances (6 sections + hero chip).
  **Why it matters**: this is the fastest way for a visually literate visitor (or a competing association's site) to pattern-match "template," undercutting the "not a generic landing page" positioning PRODUCT.md explicitly asks for.
  **Fix**: drop the label from most sections; keep at most one, reserved for a section that's genuinely a labeled category (e.g. the gallery filters already do this job for Photos).
  **Suggested command**: `/impeccable typeset`

- **[P1] Left-border stripe accents on hero-badge and wishlist-notification**: matches the skill's explicit "side-stripe borders" ban, confirmed by the detector (`side-tab`, 2 hits) — the gallery caption band uses the same treatment as a third instance.
  **Why it matters**: another recognizable AI-scaffold tell, and it was nearly re-documented as a deliberate "signature" pattern in the DESIGN.md just generated for this project — worth correcting before it calcifies into doctrine.
  **Fix**: replace the border-left with a full border, a background tint, or drop it — the gold accent can still read through icon/color instead.
  **Suggested command**: `/impeccable polish`

- **[P1] The 14-card animation grid has no chunking at the primary decision point**: violates the ≤4-item working-memory rule right where the primary persona (a busy mairie/organizer) needs to form a quick impression.
  **Why it matters**: the reassurance section built specifically for this persona ("Pour les mairies et organisateurs") sits *after* the heaviest, least-curated block on the page — if she bounces at the wall of cards, she never reaches the pitch written for her.
  **Fix**: group the 14 cards by theme (e.g. "Vie quotidienne," "Armes & justice," "Ateliers famille") or add a filter row like the gallery already has.
  **Suggested command**: `/impeccable distill`

- **[P1] Lightbox lacks the prev/next navigation the module modal has**: same "browse media" task, two divergent interaction contracts.
  **Why it matters**: a user methodically working through a photo category has to close and reopen the lightbox for each image, one at a time.
  **Fix**: port the module modal's prev/next arrow + arrow-key pattern into the lightbox.
  **Suggested command**: `/impeccable harden`

- **[P2] Low-contrast fire-red text on cream (4.1:1, needs 4.5:1)**: confirmed by the detector, affecting every eyebrow label and the hero badge liseré color sitewide.
  **Why it matters**: fails WCAG AA body-text contrast; every eyebrow across every section is affected, not a one-off.
  **Fix**: darken `--fire` slightly or use it only at large/bold sizes (≥18px), never for the small eyebrow label.
  **Suggested command**: `/impeccable harden`

- **[P2] Back-to-top button overlaps body copy and crowds the mobile bottom nav**: confirmed at 390px — the floating button sits on top of the organisers' checklist and nearly touches the bottom nav.
  **Fix**: reposition above the bottom nav with margin, or hide it while the bottom nav is visible.
  **Suggested command**: `/impeccable polish`

- **[P2] Body line-length runs ~89 characters**, above the skill's 65-75ch cap, on the wider intro/description paragraphs.
  **Fix**: constrain paragraph max-width in the intro/section-title containers.
  **Suggested command**: `/impeccable layout`

- **[P3] Module modal reuses the low-res thumb image instead of the full-size asset**, despite `assets/img/full/` variants existing for most animations.
  **Fix**: point the modal's image element at the `full/` asset when one exists.
  **Suggested command**: `/impeccable polish`

#### Minor Observations

The "Détails" badge on each card is a small gray pill — functional but easy to miss as a click affordance since the whole card is the real target. Format tabs work cleanly with correct `aria-selected` toggling. Gallery filter active-state (gold pill) is clear and immediate. Four elements (hero-card, format-card, contact-card, intro-banner) pair a hairline border with a very wide, soft shadow (`gpt-thin-border-wide-shadow`, 4 hits) — not flagged as a priority issue here, but worth a look during the polish pass since it's a recognizable AI-look combination.
